## 2. "Seeing inside" — the cut-face cap illusion (most important)
Right at the cut frame each piece must show a **broad cross-section face**, not a thin strip. Try (and record results for) at least:
 a) **Book-open tilt**: each piece gets a short pseudo-3D rotation about the cut line: scale the skin perpendicular to the cut by cos(θ) while revealing a cap polygon (the same piece outline, flattened toward the cut line) textured with the xsec texture + material shading; θ eases 0→~35° over ~120 ms then relaxes as the piece tumbles.
 b) **Thick strip**: extruded strip of width ∝ piece size along the cut edge with gradient (cheap fallback).
Pick what reads best in the 33 ms frame strips; keep the other as a quality fallback for hordes (LOD: hordes use (b), single/boss use (a)).
- Wax cap: thin glossy pink shell rim + cream core (two-layer). Jelly cap: glossy translucent lavender with a bright specular line.
- A bright **cut-line flash** (additive, 1–2 frames) exactly along the swipe on the cut frame.

## 3. Weighty physics & timing
- Pieces: separation impulse along ± cut normal ∝ swipe speed, plus a share of the swipe direction; spin; **gravity**; pieces fall off-screen (or bounce once on a floor near the bottom and settle for the boss). Small pieces tumble more.
- Hit-stop scaled by cut mass (single big cut 45–70 ms, horde 25–40 ms but the wave makes it feel big), small zoom punch, no shake > 6 px.
- **Wax**: on cut, shell cracks spread from the cut line (reuse Voronoi crack growth, ~80 ms) and shell chips peel around the cut edge; on press/hold: 予兆 per profile (squash + crack growth + `strain` emitted) then release.
- **Jelly**: make the trembling *obvious*: cut-face vertices ripple (visible wave travelling along the face), each half squash-stretches 2–3 visible cycles over ~0.8 s; amplitude large enough that frames 33 ms apart clearly differ. Add a brief "drag dent" (予兆) as the blade enters: the outline dents along the blade for 1–2 frames before splitting.
- Use the debris FX sprites where relevant (beads, drops, chips…). Wax chips: generate from `wax_shell` texture as small curved polygons.

## 4. Breaking wave (horde)
- When one swipe hits N bodies, order them by projection onto the swipe path; tier wave length: N=1:0, 2–4: 40–80 ms, 5–12: 120–220 ms, 13–25: 220–350 ms, 26+: 300–450 ms. Each body's *visual* fracture (cut, flash, pieces) happens at its dt (pending queue processed in the frame loop) — the blade trail stays visible across the wave. Emit `wave` once with all dt's, and per-body `cut`/`kill` at their own dt.
- For tiers ≥ avalanche add a final accent visual at the end of the wave (soft white shock ring along the swipe + burst of material debris) and let the tail particles linger (post phase).
- Horde test layout: scattered natural crowd (not a diagonal line): 40 grunts mixed sizes 26–40 radius, random positions in lower 75 % with small gaps.
